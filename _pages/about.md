I am a bioinformatician working on human genomics, multi-omics and machine learning. At the [ANZAC Research Institute](https://www.anzac.edu.au/) (University of Sydney) I work with Prof. Marina Kennerson's group on inherited peripheral neuropathies such as Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease: finding structural variants in short- and long-read genomes, building the group's variant-calling pipelines on the NCI Gadi supercomputer and a GPU server, and ranking candidate genes from single-cell expression data.

Before this I worked on kidney transplant genomics at the Westmead Institute for Medical Research, on prostate cancer microbiomes in the Ancestry and Health Genomics Laboratory, on microbial and soil genomics at CSIRO and Western Sydney University, and on wheat and Brassica genomes during my Ph.D. at The University of Queensland.

I care about results that can be checked: pipelines with recorded provenance, benchmarks against known truth sets such as GIAB, and analysis plans fixed before the data are seen. Most of my recent code is open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/taowis).

**Research interests:** structural variants in rare disease, clinical bioinformatics and pipeline validation, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, machine learning for genomic prediction, reproducible workflows on HPC (Nextflow, PBS, containers).
