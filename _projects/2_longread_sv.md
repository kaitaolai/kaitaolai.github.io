For one CMT family that short-read sequencing could not solve, I analysed PacBio HiFi and Oxford Nanopore genomes on NCI Gadi: SV calling with Sniffles2 and cuteSV, CpG methylation, tandem repeats and Fiber-seq chromatin data, followed by a systematic review of 45 supported candidate alleles against ClinVar and MANE transcripts.
