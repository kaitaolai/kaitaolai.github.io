PNPAtlas joins public single-nucleus data from sensory and autonomic ganglia and peripheral nerve, places the cell states in tissue with spatial data (44 Visium sections, 3 Xenium panels), and estimates them in 670 GTEx tibial nerve samples. Adding its features to PNPrank improved the historical test from AUROC 0.771 to 0.778 (p = 0.031), under a rule fixed before the test.

Gene lookup: [taowis.github.io/pnatlas](https://taowis.github.io/pnatlas/). Code: [github.com/taowis/pnatlas](https://github.com/taowis/pnatlas).
