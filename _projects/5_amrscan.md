AMRScan is a Nextflow workflow that runs AMRFinderPlus in a pinned container and writes a provenance record for every sample. It processed 10,521 *Escherichia coli* assemblies without a failed call, and 1,000 reruns gave byte-identical output.

Code: [github.com/taowis/amrscan](https://github.com/taowis/amrscan). Preprint: [arXiv:2507.08062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08062).
