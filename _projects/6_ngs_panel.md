A 31-gene hereditary cancer panel pipeline validated following AMP/CAP guidance, with the acceptance criteria fixed before testing and mapped against NPAAC and ISO 15189:2022. All 316 panel SNVs across the GIAB genomes HG001 to HG005 were found, a risk register covers 18 failure modes, and a DeepVariant update was verified from the exported VCFs alone.

Code and report: [github.com/taowis/ngs-panel-validation](https://github.com/taowis/ngs-panel-validation).
